Partnership Approvals: Who Gets the Say

Every collaboration enters through Marketing. The deal's tier decides who approves it. The Marketing lead has the final say on anything with money, joint branding or long-term commitment.

Approval tiers

Tier 1 · Small collabNo money, no joint branding, one campaign or less. Example: a one-off creator post.
PARTNER OWNER DECIDES · 2 WORKING DAYS
Tier 2 · Standard partnershipPaid within the campaign budget, or joint branding. Example: an event sponsor.
MARKETING LEAD DECIDES · 5 WORKING DAYS
Tier 3 · Strategic partnershipOver budget, exclusivity, several campaigns, or shared customer data.
MARKETING LEAD, AFTER LEGAL + FINANCE · 10 DAYS
Tie-breaksUnsure of the tier? Go one tier up. If people disagree, the Marketing lead decides and that call is final. A sub-lead can say "we can't deliver" but can't approve a deal.
