Handoffs between sub-departments

Events→SocialDates, speakers and visuals at stage 3, so the content calendar is built around the event.
Social→EventsRegistration link placement, hashtag and on-site shot list before stage 4.
Events⇄SocialStage 6: photos and quotes flow out in real time; attendee questions route back.

Rules that keep it moving

  • One brief, one budget, one calendar, all owned by Marketing. No second source of truth.
  • Approvals only at the gates. Inside a stage, sub-leads decide.
  • Date or budget changes go to Marketing first, then to both subs the same day.
  • Every campaign ends with a report, even small ones. Reports make the next brief faster.
