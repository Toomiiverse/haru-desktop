# Complete the emotion classifier's vocabulary

`determined` and `worried` were valid output labels but had no definitions in the
classification prompt. The other ten labels did. The prompt now defines both
missing meanings, using the exact text evaluated with Haru's current local model.

## Controlled local-model check

432 isolated calls tested the original and completed prompts on 48 invented
dialogue lines plus six reported-emotion challenges. Each prompt ran in calm and
irritated contexts, with two fixed seeds. These calls did not enter Haru's chat
history, move her actual mood, or produce speech.

The first two lines per emotion were development examples; the other two were
held out. The held-out figures below cover **24 distinct lines**, repeated across
two mood contexts and two seeds. Repetitions are not independent new examples.

| Held-out expected-label agreement | Original | Completed definitions |
|---|---:|---:|
| Calm context | 40/48 | 48/48 |
| Irritated context | 38/48 | 44/48 |
| Combined | 78/96 (81.25%) | 92/96 (95.83%) |

Both prompts remained parseable. The completed prompt fixed the held-out
determination/worry confusions, but introduced two repeated disagreements on one
happy line in the irritated context. It did not improve the separate challenge
where lines merely report someone else's emotion; existing mood context strongly
influenced those labels.

Expected labels were authored for this engineering test, not independently rated
by people. This measures a classifier component, before tone sharpening and face
rendering, on the current local Qwen3.5-9B configuration. It does not establish
better conversations, a more appealing personality, or biological emotion.

## External regression check

A later frozen-prompt check used 84 human-labelled public GoEmotions comments,
mapped into seven overlapping categories, with the same two moods and two seeds
(672 calls). Agreement rose from 86/336 (25.60%) to 106/336 (31.55%), primarily
through worry recognition. There were 32 paired corrections and 12 regressions.
The supplied irritated mood substantially reduced agreement under both prompts.

Reddit comments and companion delivery have different contexts, and the mapped,
balanced sample is not the full dataset's benchmark. Still, the low agreement
shows that the invented-line 95.83% result is not general emotion accuracy. The
production prompt was unchanged; a separate offline mood-context follow-up uses
a different split. See the [full report](../research/fruitfly/REPORT.md)
and [public-check results](../research/fruitfly/goemotions-results.json).

That follow-up used 128 previously unused comments and retained six earlier
challenge lines as a separate probe (1,608 calls). Public-label agreement was
21.68% with the current prompt, 26.95% when explicit wording took priority, and
35.94% without the background mood sentence. Most of the latter gain came from
avoiding the irritated/high-ego context; calm agreement barely changed. Overall
agreement remained weak, and desired character delivery was not rated. Neither
candidate was deployed. [Ablation results](../research/fruitfly/mood-ablation-results.json)

## Reproduce

After `npm test`, create the fixed comparison job:

    node research/fruitfly/prepare-emotion-job.cjs emotion-job.json

On the Haru server, with its existing local Ollama provider:

    node classify-emotion-job.cjs emotion-job.json emotion-results.jsonl

The runner refuses non-local providers and an existing output file. It reads the
configured model and endpoint; no conversation or memory is loaded. The summary
is generated by `research/fruitfly/emotion-report.cjs`.
